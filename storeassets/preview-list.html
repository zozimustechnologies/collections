The Future of Web Research and Note-Taking

Visual Research Tools

Modern knowledge workers need smarter tools to capture and organise information from across the web. Whether you're researching a topic, curating content, or just saving things to read later, the ability to collect contextual snippets — not just bare links — makes all the difference.

Browser-native sidebar panels have changed the way we interact with web content. Instead of juggling bookmarks folders or third-party apps, the best tools live right inside the browser, in context, as you browse.

The ability to save a quote alongside its source URL, attach an image, and write a quick note — all in a single organised collection — represents a fundamentally better research workflow.
